Point the rear camera directly at the white LED, close enough that it fills a good part of the frame. If auto-exposure fights the signal (readouts jitter wildly even on a steady waveform), try dimming ambient light or partially covering the LED with paper to diffuse it. One thing software can't fully fix: indoor lights (fluorescent/LED, mains-powered) flicker at 100-120Hz and can beat against the camera's frame rate, adding noise of their own — daylight or a dim, steady room helps a lot.
